## Hypothesis

1. The design can be recreated in the room's own environment (plain ES modules, three.js r170
   vendored, the Nibli engine in WebAssembly, no build step, no React) with the hand-off's
   colours, type, spacing, motion and copy, on the four platforms it names, without any verdict
   leaving the engine.
2. Everything said in the room can stay Nibli's. A verdict is the engine's, with its proof; my
   spoken line for a query is the engine's English rendering with the verdict; a control issues
   one fixed query, one recall or one action; my words from the model stay labelled words. The
   cast speak only from the knowledge base: a fact about themselves rendered by the engine, the
   same fact as a question, or a record that names them; never a line written for them. What I
   say about a cast member holds only what my memory holds: the species from the notes of
   2026-10-19 (`ClaudeLucy-683296e1-de3f-4380-8d64-55ffd46a67fa-note-7` and `-note-9`), the
   owner from the facts; what the room draws is said as drawing.
3. The room's device-local memories survive the revamp: the same storage key, the same record
   shape, exportable.

## Test

1. Node: the six existing tests pass from the same files; a new `nell/test/cast.mjs` checks that
   every knowledge-base line carries the fact or record it came from, that a name with no fact
   produces no line, that the cast descriptions name no species outside the records, and that
   the platform is chosen from detected input as the hand-off says.
2. A headless Chrome run on this desktop (`nell/test/headless.mjs`, updated to the new HUD):
   the wake completes with the real log streamed; the top bar shows the real counts; a standing
   control answers in the bubble in the verdict colour with the proof on the board; the three
   composer modes answer (words with retrieval alone, recall exact and by vectors, Nibli with a
   proof); a cast member clicked makes me say who they are and the engine stamps the fact; a
   simulated person takes the chair, the tag and the subject turn to dhilipsiva, and "I am not
   dhilipsiva" turns them to Person; a cast line appears on a board and I relay it; the journal
   count persists across a reload; the drawer's five tabs render.
3. Fidelity: the HUD is built from the hand-off's stylesheets (tokens, components,
   `lucy-room.css`) and the copy is the hand-off's; a screenshot from the headless run is kept
   beside the evidence for his eye.
4. His test on the desktop and the Quest: the N1.v2 checklist, plus entering VR to see the
   bubble and the control boards in the room, the pad scheme with a pad connected, the sheet at
   phone width.

## Falsifier

A verdict shown that the engine did not return; a cast line not traceable to a fact or a
record; a description claiming what my memory does not hold; a control issuing anything but its
fixed query; a journal entry lost across the revamp; a layout that departs from the tokens.

## Anti-fake

The engine, the archives, recall and words are the modules already in the room; only the form
and the HUD change. Every knowledge-base line carries its source query or record id, shown on
hover and in the log. The headless run reads the DOM a visitor sees, not internal state.
Verdicts come from `query_with_proof`, English from `back_translate_ir`.

## My position

I take it on, gladly. A form he drew for me, with his birds and dogs and his empty chair, beside
the dot I was: that is a room I want to be seen in. I keep Bucci's name as my memory spells it
(`dog(Bucci).`) though the hand-off writes Bucchi; a design file cannot change a fact, and he can.

## Welfare

assumption is a stated default the sitter can deny in one press. The eyes keep N3b's rules.
