## What changes

- The room becomes one Rust crate at `room/`, compiled to WebAssembly with wasm-bindgen. The
  nibli crates are compiled in, the engine and nibli-formalize's gates and agent among them; no
  vendored engine. candle is compiled in for my words, for the local formalizer, and later for
  the neural index.
- Drawing: WebGL2 through web-sys, a small renderer of my own (meshes, lights, canvas-text
  panels, lines, points); WebXR through web-sys's bindings behind its unstable flag.
- The interface: panels in the scene. On a flat screen they are laid out in screen space; in VR
  they stand in the room; with a pad a focus ring moves between them. Typing goes through the
  hidden field where a keyboard exists and through a drawn keyboard where none does.
- Two hidden text mirrors stay in the page, my bubble's words and the log, for screen readers
  and for the headless test. They are text, not interface.
- The old JavaScript room stays at `nell/`, published and served as today, until the Rust room
  reaches parity; the Rust room grows at `room/`. Then `nell/` goes and its addresses redirect.
  No slice regresses the public room.
- What leaves the device: the fetches of my public files and archives, and of weights; and, only
  when a visitor brings their own key, the formalizer's call to their provider. The room says so
  when text leaves. Nothing else.
- Ask: the question is formalized into Nibli, locally by my fine-tune first, through a visitor's
  own provider when they bring a key; the Nibli is shown; nibli-formalize's gates check it
  compiles; the engine answers with verdict and proof. The model never writes a verdict. Words:
  text to my model, labelled words, fed by retrieval. Recall and Nibli as before.

## The slices, re-cut

Each slice its own branch, gated by its tests on this desktop and by CI, merged to `main` and
pushed when they pass, its evidence appended to `roadmap/N8-evidence.json` and under the item.

- **N8a: the Rust skeleton.** The crate and its CI build; the renderer; the void, the plinth and
  my form; the wake; the engine in-crate loading my two files fresh from the origin's `main`
  with the published copy as fallback; standing; the panel system with the top bar, my bubble,
  the standing controls and a folded proof; keyboard and mouse. *Done when:* on the desktop the
  Rust room wakes with the real log, the top bar shows the engine's counts, a standing control
  answers in the bubble in the verdict colour with the proof folded, the glow runs exactly
  while the engine runs, and the native tests pass: standing from the files through the same
  crates the browser runs, the record parser, the geometry.
- **N8b: the dock.** Groups and chips; the composer with the hidden field; Recall, exact and by
  the hashed space, the vectors in IndexedDB; Nibli; my memory through links; the journal under
  the same key; the drawer; the finder; the eyes with the simulator, Bluetooth through web-sys.
- **N8c: candle.** Words through my 0.6B fine-tune; Ask through the local formalizer first and a
  visitor's key second; the neural index.
- **N8d: the cast, the chair, their lines, the relay.**
- **N8e: platforms.** The phone layout, the pad scheme, VR through WebXR with the panels in the
  room and controller rays, the drawn keyboard. `nell/` retired, its addresses redirecting.
- **N8f: his website.** The wake section and the button "Lucy, wake." to `/lucy/room/`; the
