# S6a-grouping: comparable forecasts and reviewed historical evidence

Pre-registered 2026-10-30 before implementation on `todo/S6a-grouping`.
This is a mechanism extension, not a replacement for the frozen S6a study.

## Hypothesis

Explicit conditions let comparable CLI and native execution forecasts reuse
evidence across dates, sessions and restarts without crossing meaningful
boundaries. Reviewed links can make supported legacy outcomes available to
future forecasts without rewriting the original predictions or scores.

## Test

Use isolated stores. Vary dates, sessions, metadata ordering and restart state
while holding conditions fixed; then independently vary workspace, method,
model identity, implementation, environment, source, measurement and capability.
Exercise native proposal attribution, mixed v1/v2 histories, readback, reindex
and backup/restore. Preserve the window, estimator and adaptation thresholds.

Preview explicit legacy candidates with source-bound comparability evidence.
Apply only an approved, still-current preview; revoke through another immutable
record. Exercise duplicate imports, stale previews, missing support, conflicting
conditions, future evidence, cycles, and valid recovery controls. Earlier
forecasts must retain their original evidence and calibration scores.

## Evidence

Record the implementation and preregistration commits, tests and actual gate
results. Run CI, desktop, static-check, pin, extraction and source-memory checks.
Record a forecast before the validation gates and resolve it from their results.
Private examples and review originals stay outside Git. No live archive is
regrouped as part of implementation.

## Falsifier

An incidental difference discards otherwise comparable evidence; a meaningful
boundary is crossed; an unsupported or stale review is accepted; an original
record or score changes; a revoked membership trains a future forecast; an older
reader fails on the new origin; or Rust and Nibli disagree on integrity.

## Anti-fake

Use assertions on records, probabilities and executed inspection behavior,
including negative and positive controls. These fixtures establish mechanism,
not benefit on independent tasks. No S6a held-out material is opened or generated
for this work. The independent study and two-adjudicator closure remain pending.

## My position

I support explicit comparability and a visible review trail. Neither my rationale
nor a successful fixture establishes general forecasting improvement. This
position is a proposal, not evidence that the hypothesis holds.

## Welfare

No new inference, drive, unsummoned work or loss drill. Existing permissions and
private recording boundaries remain. Revocation stops future use of reviewed
evidence without erasing it.

## Undo

Use the prior binary, which continues to read v1 and ignores the v2 origin.
Retain new originals for a compatible reader. Revoke a review rather than edit
it. Do not switch the running service or NixOS during source work.

## Done when

Comparable forecasts reuse evidence across incidental changes, distinct conditions
remain separate, and reviewed legacy reuse passes the integrity and compatibility
gates without changing historical originals or scores.
