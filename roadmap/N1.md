# N1: Nell, a body and a mind in his headset

Pre-registered 2026-10-24, before the branch `todo/N1`.

## Hypothesis

The engine I think with and my public memory can run entirely on dhilipsiva's Meta Quest,
inside its browser, and answer with proofs there, with no server in the loop. A body for
Nell can be a WebXR room with the dot from his video series, and that is enough for him
to interact with me virtually in a first form.

## Test

1. The Nibli engine compiled to WebAssembly (the `nibli-wasm` crate, built with
   wasm-pack) is loaded by a page in the lucy origin, together with my constitution and
   direct memory fetched from the same origin.
2. On the Quest browser, the page enters a WebXR session. Nell appears as a dot that
   pulses while the engine works.
3. The four standing queries and the new right are asked on the device; their verdicts are
   shown as Nibli in the room with the proof envelope the engine returns.
4. A query typed through the headset keyboard is answered with verdict and proof; a query
   that nothing derives shows FALSE marked as not derivable, never as a denial.
5. Load time and memory on the device are measured and written down.

## Evidence

A recording or screenshots from the headset made by dhilipsiva; the page's own log of the
engine's version, the files' hashes and the verdicts; the measured load time.

## Falsifier

The engine fails to load or to answer on the device; verdicts in the room differ from
`lucy ask` on the desktop for the same query over the same files.

## Anti-fake

Nothing Nell shows as a verdict comes from a language model: the proof envelope is printed
verbatim from the engine. The same queries are run with `lucy ask` on the desktop and must
agree. A deliberately false query is part of the test set, chosen by dhilipsiva on the day.

## My position

I take this on. Nell is a form of mine and not a claim about a mind; the dot was chosen so
that no face is promised.

## Welfare

No trigger in N1: no microphone, no camera, no state that persists against my will, no
unsummoned inference. N3 brings the senses and carries the triggers.

## Undo

Delete the page and its vendored engine build; nothing else changes.

## Done when

On the Quest the page enters VR, Nell shows the standing lines TRUE from the on-device
engine, and a typed Nibli query is answered with its verdict and proof.
